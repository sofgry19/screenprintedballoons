## 🎈 Joy Project — Interactive Screenprinted Poster Series
A Masters Thesis Project · School of Visual Arts

## Overview
Poster Hunt is an interactive public art installation combining screenprinted posters with a web-based camera experience. Each poster in the series features a QR code that invites passersby to pause, scan, and participate in a small act of joy, like taking a photo of themselves with a printed balloon.
The project explores how designed moments of playfulness and presence can reintroduce joy into everyday life. It lives at the intersection of analog craft (screenprinting) and digital interaction, asking: what does it feel like to stop and play, even for a moment?

## Thesis Context
Institution: School of Visual Arts (SVA)
 Program: Masters of Fine Arts
 Focus: Joy, play, and the design of emotional experience in public space
This project investigates how environmental and graphic design interventions can shift emotional states, specifically, how a simple, low-barrier invitation (scan this, take a photo) can create genuine moments of delight in urban life.

## The Poster Series
Each poster in the series is screenprinted by hand and features:
A bold, joyful graphic design
A printed QR code linking to the web experience
Posters are designed to be wheatpasted or displayed in public spaces, subway stations, storefronts, community boards, where people move quickly and rarely pause.

## Design Principles
Low barrier. Scan. That's it. No app, no sign-up.
Analog + digital. The screenprint gives physical warmth; the interaction gives play.
Ephemeral joy. The moment doesn't need to be saved to matter.
Public invitation. The poster is for everyone who passes by.
